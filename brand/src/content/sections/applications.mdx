<Block label="Before you publish" text="Ten questions for anything that carries the hzero name.">
  <Grid cols={2}>
    <Card kind="do" title="Check">
      <ul>
        <li>Does it say one thing, in one sentence?</li>
        <li>Is hzero written in lowercase everywhere in the text?</li>
        <li>At most one cyan element on this screen, slide or page?</li>
        <li>Cyan only on charcoal, deep cyan on light?</li>
        <li>Does every text colour pass the contrast table?</li>
      </ul>
    </Card>
    <Card kind="do" title="Check">
      <ul>
        <li>Titles in FK Display, text in Inter, nothing in monospace?</li>
        <li>Does it pass the slop test?</li>
        <li>Disruptive results, without threatening disruption?</li>
        <li>Every photo real, every chart made with hzero_charts?</li>
        <li>Would an engineer on the team say it out loud?</li>
      </ul>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>
