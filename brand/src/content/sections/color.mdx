<Block label="Colour inspiration" text="Where the palette comes from.">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>The cyan is the signal: rare, deliberate and recognisable, even on a copied diagram with no logo on it. "You don't see that colour often anywhere else." Every grey carries a trace of it, so the palette feels like one family, and the page itself is black with a trace of cyan, never pure #000.</p>
    <p>Click any HEX value to copy it. All values live in one file, tokens/hzero.json, which the website and the chart package follow.</p>
  </div>
</Block>

<Block label="Proportion" text="The balance of colour keeps the identity calm. Use this mix on any page, slide or screen.">
  <Proportion />
  <div class="prose"><p>The cyan is about 3% of any page: one highlight per screen or slide. If two things are cyan, one of them shouldn't be. The exception is a large cyan field with charcoal line drawings, used rarely for a cover or a poster.</p></div>
</Block>

<Block label="Print" text="The cyan cannot be reproduced in standard four-colour print.">
  <Grid cols={3}>
    <Card title="Spot colour">Print the cyan as a spot colour, chosen against a printed proof, or put it on charcoal or black stock where its brightness reads. <Tag kind="todo" /></Card>
    <Card title="On white paper">Use deep cyan or charcoal. Never the bright cyan.</Card>
    <Card title="Cards and merchandise">Charcoal or black base, the logo or the h-mark in cyan.</Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>

<Block label="Do and don't" text="The most common mistakes.">
  <Grid cols={2}>
    <Card kind="do" title="Do">
      <ul><li>One cyan accent per screen, on charcoal.</li><li>Greys first, colour last.</li><li>Deep cyan whenever the background is light.</li><li>Signal green for connectors in diagrams and the highlighted part of a section title.</li></ul>
    </Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Don't">
      <ul><li>Thin cyan text on white.</li><li>Cyan and green in the same element.</li><li>Dark teal with orange text and monospace type: it reads as AI-generated.</li><li>Gradients from green to blue, rainbow palettes, beige with blue.</li></ul>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>
