<Block label="What we do" text="The plain description. Use it as it is.">
  <Statement>hzero makes quantum and classical computers work as one system. Its software orchestrates workloads, quantum computers and HPC resources together, inside the tools an HPC centre already runs. We call this <em>operational integration</em>.</Statement>
</Block>

<Block label="Mission" text="A clear goal we work towards every day. Drafted from the brand session; to confirm.">
  <Statement eyebrow="Proposal">Make quantum and classical computers work as one system, inside the centres that run them today.</Statement>
</Block>

<Block label="Vision" text="The future we want to create. Drafted from the brand session; to confirm.">
  <Statement eyebrow="Proposal">Give the people who run computing centres control they never had, so hybrid computing does more with what already exists.</Statement>
</Block>

<Block label="Essence" text="The one idea that kept coming back in the session.">
  <Statement>Disruptive results. <em>Zero disruption.</em></Statement>
  <div class="prose"><p>hzero changes what an HPC centre can do with its quantum and classical machines, without asking it to change how it works.</p></div>
</Block>

<Block label="Principles" text="Three rules everything hzero makes must follow. Each comes with a test a designer or writer can check.">
  <Grid cols={3}>
    <Card eyebrow="Pillar 1" title="Works with what you have">
      <p>hzero fits into the HPC centre's existing tools and schedulers, and changes what the system can do. The disruption is in the result, never in the process.</p>
      <p><strong>Always</strong> show hzero next to the tools operators already use. <strong>Never</strong> "replace", "rip out" or "revolution".</p>
    </Card>
    <Card eyebrow="Pillar 2" title="Complexity in, clarity out">
      <p>hzero takes on the full complexity of a hybrid system and hands the operator a few clear controls. Knobs, not a black box.</p>
      <p><strong>Always</strong> one clear number, one control, one message per screen. <strong>Never</strong> dump every metric on screen.</p>
    </Card>
    <Card eyebrow="Pillar 3" title="Grown-up from day one">
      <p>Mature in product and in brand: rigorous, commercial, and crafted like the company hzero intends to become.</p>
      <p><strong>Always</strong> real results, real customers, rigorous charts. <strong>Never</strong> look like a research project with a website.</p>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
  <Quote by="Martin">We hide the complexity of everything else and we give you knobs to turn.</Quote>
</Block>

<Block label="Values" text="How hzero behaves. Rules each person uses on themselves, not a stick to hit others with.">
  <Grid cols={3}>
    <Card title="Say it once, say it short">One message per slide, section or chart. If it needs a second message, it needs a second slide.</Card>
    <Card title="Show the system working as one">Diagrams, motion and words make the hybrid system read as one whole, with hzero listening to every part.</Card>
    <Card title="Good defaults, open controls">"Our default setup should be what we think is good, and then they can go break it." Ship an opinion, never a lock.</Card>
    <Card title="Rigour is part of the look">Charts are readable for everyone, including colour-blind readers, and still recognisably hzero.</Card>
    <Card title="The best idea wins, the owner decides">Everyone gives input, one person decides: whoever holds the torch. Like a rowing boat: everyone pulls, one sets the pace.</Card>
    <Card title="Real over staged">Candid photos of the actual team at work. No stock smiles, no glowing qubits.</Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>

<Block label="Writing rules" text="The details that make hzero sound like hzero.">
  <div class="rows">
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">The name</p><div class="row-text"><p>Always hzero: one word, all lowercase, also at the start of a sentence. Never hZero, HZero, H0 or Hzero. The logo is the only place the name appears in capitals. The legal entity is hzero GmbH; use it only in legal and contact contexts.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Our words</p><div class="row-text"><p>operational integration (lowercase, our term), work as one, orchestrate, control, knobs, hybrid system, operators, workloads.</p><p class="muted">"I want this term that we coined, operational integration, to be splattered everywhere." Bruno</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Retired words</p><div class="row-text"><p>bridge, glue, revolution, game-changing, unlock the full potential, seamless, cutting-edge, leverage, and next-generation on its own.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">The slop test</p><div class="row-text"><p>If any company in the market could publish the sentence unchanged, rewrite it. Then push twice: which customer, which machine, what changed?</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Numbers</p><div class="row-text"><p>Real numbers with units. Figures for all numbers above nine. Percentages as "99.5%". Ranges with an en dash: "2–5 seconds".</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Titles</p><div class="row-text"><p>Sentence case, no full stop, ideally under eight words. US English, as on the website.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">AI as a tool</p><div class="row-text"><p>AI drafts are fine. Nothing ships until it passes the slop test and sounds like an engineer on the team would say it out loud.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</Block>
