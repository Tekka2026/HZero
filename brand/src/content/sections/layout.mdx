<Block label="Applying the logo" text="Size and placement once a grid is set.">
  <div class="rows">
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Scale</p><div class="row-text"><p>On the website the logo is one column wide. On slides, about 1.5× the margin wide. Below 96 px, use the h-mark.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Placement</p><div class="row-text"><p>Top left on pages and slides, or centred on a closing slide. When in doubt, left align.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</Block>

<Block label="Applying type" text="Hierarchy and placement of titles and text.">
  <div class="rows">
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Scale</p><div class="row-text"><p>Titles are always clearly larger than the logo. At most three type sizes on one slide.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Placement</p><div class="row-text"><p>Left aligned, starting top left. Centre only a single closing statement.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">One message per unit</p><div class="row-text"><p>One idea per slide, website section or chart. If it needs a second message, it needs a second slide.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</Block>

<Block label="Motion" text="Motion shows control: things arrive, settle and stay.">
  <div class="rows">
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Easing</p><div class="row-text"><p>Expo-out, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Fast start, long gentle landing. No bounce.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Duration</p><div class="row-text"><p>Interface 150–300 ms. Content reveals around 1.2–1.6 s. Diagrams build part by part.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Titles</p><div class="row-text"><p>Words rise one by one from a mask with a slight turn, starting at 35% opacity.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Restraint</p><div class="row-text"><p>One moving thing at a time. No scroll-jacking. Everything works with reduced motion.</p></div></div>
