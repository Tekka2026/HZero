<Block label="Background" text="What the logo is made of, and why." action="Download all logos" href="downloads/hzero-logos.zip">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>The logo has two parts: the h-mark, an open slanted bracket around the H, and the italic ZERO wordmark. Together they say what hzero does: one frame that holds different systems. The bracket is always lighter than the letters.</p>
    <p>The founders "adopted it, not married to it". A refinement is being explored; until it is approved, these rules apply to the current logo. The lowercase name in text is a fixed decision.</p>
  </div>
</Block>
