<Block label="Background" text="What the logo is made of, and why." action="Download all logos" href="downloads/hzero-logos.zip">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>The logo has two parts: the h-mark, a solid slanted block with a lowercase h cut out of it, and the lowercase zero wordmark. Both share the same slant and the same chamfered corners, so they read as one shape.</p>
    <p>The name is lowercase everywhere, also in the logo. The logo is always one colour; the h shows the background through the block.</p>
  </div>
</Block>
