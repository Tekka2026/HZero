<Block label="Ethos" text="What our photography is for.">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>hzero's product mostly runs in the background, so photography carries the human side: the team building it, the machines it runs, the calm order it creates. Candid, non-staged photos: "it feels spontaneous, it feels creative".</p>
    <p>The photos on this page come from the founders' photo selection: the 19 of 64 that both Bruno and Martin marked as hzero. They are mood references for briefing photographers, not licensed for publication.</p>
  </div>
</Block>

<Block label="Principles" text="The feeling words both founders used across the photos.">
  <Grid cols={2}>
    <Card kind="do" title="Our photography is">
      <ul><li>Calm</li><li>Ordered</li><li>Candid</li><li>Crafted</li><li>Human</li></ul>
    </Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Our photography isn't">
      <ul><li>Staged</li><li>Stock</li><li>Generated</li><li>Messy</li><li>Glowing "quantum"</li></ul>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>

<Block label="Photo buckets" text="Imagery falls into three categories. Each has its own rules.">
  <div class="rows">
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">People</p><div class="row-text"><p>The real team, at work, in conversation, mid-thought. Natural light, not looking at the camera, everyone in their own clothes. Working together like a rowing crew.</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Product and hardware</p><div class="row-text"><p>Machines, server blades, cables, control rooms: calm, ordered, encapsulated complexity. No people in product shots: "once it's there, it just does its thing."</p></div></div>
    <div class="row"><p class="row-name">Places</p><div class="row-text"><p>Controlled, clean, lived-in: a workshop where things get built, a calm operations room. Not a messy desk, not a sterile studio.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</Block>

<Block label="Shoot brief" text="For the next team gathering, north of the Arctic Circle.">
  <Grid cols={2}>
    <Card kind="do" title="Do">
      <ul><li>Hire a local photographer and shoot what happens.</li><li>Cool, neutral grading that sits with the greys.</li><li>Leave calm space in the frame for a title.</li></ul>
    </Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Don't">
      <ul><li>Stock photos, posed smiles, handshakes.</li><li>Glowing blue circuits, floating qubits, particle effects.</li><li>AI-generated people or offices.</li><li>Warm beige interiors with blue accents.</li></ul>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>
