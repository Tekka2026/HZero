<Block label="Background" text="Two families, each with one job. No monospace anywhere, not even for numbers or code-like labels.">

    <p>FK Display sets every title: confident, slightly technical, calm at large sizes. Inter sets everything people read and use, from body text to chart labels. Together they keep hzero exact without looking academic.</p>

<Block label="Don'ts" text="Do not diminish the value of typography. Avoid these treatments.">
  <Grid cols={3}>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Other typefaces">FK Display and Inter only. Plex Sans only as a fallback.</Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Monospace">Not for code-like labels, not for numbers, not for "technical" flavour.</Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Bold titles">FK Display is used in Regular only.</Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="All caps">Only for very short labels, never for titles or buttons.</Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Levels too close">More than three type sizes on one slide, or sizes too close to tell apart.</Card>
    <Card kind="dont" title="Low contrast">Text colours that fail the contrast table on the colour page.</Card>
  </Grid>
  <Grid cols={2}>
    <Card kind="do" title="Do">
      <ul><li>Sentence case for every title and button, no full stop at the end.</li><li>Tighten large titles slightly (−2 to −2.5%); leave text at 0.</li><li>Set the second line of a title in steel, or the highlighted part in signal green.</li><li>Tabular figures wherever numbers line up.</li></ul>
    </Card>
  </Grid>
</Block>
