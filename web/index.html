From connected resourcesto system-wide value

Why it matters Connecting quantum and classical resources is only the beginning. Even when individual resources perform at their best, the complete hybrid system can fall short of its potential.

Unlocking the full value of hybrid computing requires optimizing the system as a whole, with success measured by the performance it delivers to users and the economic value it creates.

How it works hzero provides operational integration across heterogeneous QC–HPC infrastructure, integrating with existing schedulers, application runtimes, and quantum software stacks.

  1. 01

    Objectives and workloads

    Operators set what the hybrid system should optimise for. Applications submit workloads with their requirements.

  2. 02

    One view of the system

    hzero combines application requirements, real-time resource states and operator-defined objectives into coordinated decisions.

  3. 03

    Coordinated assignments

    Workloads are assigned across quantum and classical resources, through the schedulers, runtimes and quantum software stacks already in place.

  4. 04

    Conditions change

    Infrastructure telemetry reports operating conditions. Here, a quantum system drops to reduced capacity.

  5. 05

    The system adapts

    hzero evaluates the change against requirements and objectives, and adapts how the hybrid system is used.
